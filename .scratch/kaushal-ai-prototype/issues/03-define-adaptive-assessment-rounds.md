# Define the adaptive assessment rounds

## Question

How many assessment rounds exist, what supplies their questions, and when may the final round run?

## Answer

Round 1 uses fixed questions tied to the official's published job-role matrix. Round 2 uses 7-10 AI-selected or AI-generated questions based on Round 1 evidence. Round 3 is optional, asks at most five questions, and runs only when no more than 80% of the matrix has supported competency results or important evidence remains contradictory.

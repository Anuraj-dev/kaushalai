# Position Kaushal AI beside iGOT AI-CBP

## Question

How does Kaushal AI relate to the designation, KCM competency, course, and approval workflow already present in iGOT AI-CBP?

## Answer

iGOT AI-CBP remains the upstream department-level workflow. Kaushal AI adds an individual evidence and feedback loop: assess an official against a published job-role matrix, identify supported gaps, and recommend learning. Seeded data supplies profiles and courses now; later adapters may replace those providers with government and iGOT APIs.
